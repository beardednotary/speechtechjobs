Speech AI salaries 2026: pay ranges from live job postings

Updated November 6, 2026 from 59 open roles at 18 speech and voice AI companies that publish pay ranges.

These are the base salary ranges employers state in their own job postings: ASR, text-to-speech, voice agents and audio ML roles. Most come from US pay-transparency rules, so they're US ranges; equity and bonus come on top. The median posted midpoint across every role is $228K.

How this data is collected

Each week we pull open roles from the career pages of the speech-AI companies we track and keep the speech, voice and audio roles. Only annual US-dollar ranges stated in the posting are used, with no estimates or self-reported numbers. The page is rebuilt every time the jobs data refreshes, so roles that close drop out.
