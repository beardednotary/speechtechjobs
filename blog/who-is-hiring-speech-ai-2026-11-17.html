Who's hiring in speech AI this week

November 17, 2026 • 22 roles

Speech AI keeps moving onto devices, and this week's biggest group of roles is about making models fit on chips, headphones and phones. Deepgram wants an engineer to write custom kernels so its models run on hardware other than NVIDIA GPUs (up to $274.1K, remote in the US). Amazon's Gdansk team builds text-to-speech that works offline, and Sonos is hiring in Glasgow for the noise cancellation and voice pickup in its headphones. Two sections are new this week. One is for linguists and speech-data people, led by an Apple role paying up to $324.8K to build the data behind live speech translation. The other is summer 2027 research internships at Amazon, Apple and Dolby, which are open now. In Europe there are openings in Glasgow, Gdansk, Oslo, Barcelona, Berlin, Cambridge, Aachen and London.

On-device, edge & DSP

Getting a speech model to run inside a power, memory and heat budget is now a job title of its own. These roles want people who can work from the model all the way down to the chip. More like these on embedded voice AI jobs.

  • Embedded AI Engineer, On-Device Models — Deepgram · Remote (US) · $219.3K–$274.1K
    Gets Deepgram's speech models running on hardware other than NVIDIA GPUs: embedded SoCs, mobile processors, DSPs and NPUs. When a vendor's runtime can't run a model well enough, you write the kernels and operators yourself in C, C++, Rust or assembly, and handle quantization, operator fusion and memory layout to meet latency, power and thermal limits. Targets include embedded Linux, bare metal and RTOS. The level (senior or staff) depends on experience. Posted in July and still open. View posting
  • Applied Scientist II, Text-to-Speech on Device — Amazon · Gdansk, Poland · compensation not listed
    Builds speech generation models that run offline on devices with little memory or compute, for Alexa, cars, and accessibility features for people who are blind or have low vision. The work runs from research to integration on real hardware, using model compression and knowledge distillation. Requires a PhD in a quantitative field and experience designing, training and pruning deep learning models. Quantization, distillation and speech-generation experience are preferred. View posting
  • Senior DSP Engineer, Wearables — Sonos · Glasgow, UK (in office 3 days a week) · compensation not listed
    Writes the signal processing that runs on Sonos headphones: active noise cancellation, transparency mode, voice pickup and Bluetooth audio. You'd turn MATLAB or Python reference models into C/C++ for fixed- and floating-point embedded DSPs, test the results bit-exact against those models, and profile on the device for cycles, memory and power. You must live within commuting distance of Glasgow. View posting
  • Member of Technical Staff, Research Engineer (Omni) — Liquid AI · Boston, MA (hybrid) · compensation not listed
    Liquid AI, an MIT CSAIL spin-out, builds models that run efficiently from data centers down to on-device hardware. Omni is its single model for vision and audio, and this role covers all of it: data, pre- and post-training, evals, and inference and deployment. It needs hands-on training experience in at least one non-text modality. The posting says audio experience is rare and can be learned on the job, and it's open to strong early-career candidates as well as experienced ones. View posting
  • Applied Scientist, Alexa Edge AI — Amazon · Bengaluru, India · compensation not listed
    A founding member of a new Alexa Edge AI team in Bangalore, building vision, audio (including speech) and multimodal models for both edge and cloud. Requires a PhD, or a master's plus 3+ years, at least a year building models for business use, and experience building speech recognition, machine translation or NLP systems. Pruning, quantization and distillation experience is preferred. View posting
  • Acoustic Lead, Security Products — Motorola Solutions · Fornebu (Oslo), Norway · compensation not listed
    Leads audio hardware design and tuning for security devices, especially two-way "talk-down" speakers that must be loud and clear outdoors. You'd set acoustic requirements, choose loudspeakers, microphones and amplifiers, design enclosures and microphone placement, do the DSP tuning, and carry it through mass-production testing with manufacturing partners. It's an acoustics role, not an ML role. Posted November 13. View posting
  • Core Audio Software Engineer — Apple · Cupertino, CA · $150.4K–$277.6K base
    Joins the audio team in Apple's Media, Graphics & Compute group, which provides the audio layer under Siri, FaceTime, media capture and playback. This opening is focused on game audio. Requires 5+ years of software development, strong C++ or Swift, real-time multithreaded programming, and performance profiling. View posting

Speech data, evaluation & linguists

Speech models are only as good as their data and their evals, and these teams hire for language skill directly. If you trained as a linguist or phonetician, start here. More on spoken language jobs.

  • Senior Software Engineer, Streaming Speech Translation Data — Apple · Cupertino, CA · $216.2K–$324.8K base
    Builds the data pipelines behind Apple's real-time speech-to-speech and speech-to-text translation models. You'd turn raw multilingual data into training and evaluation sets using LLM APIs, forced alignment, text normalization and GPU-run text-to-speech. Requires 5+ years building large-scale data pipelines or ML infrastructure in Python, and working knowledge of audio processing, forced alignment and multilingual text. Text normalization across languages and scripts is preferred, which suits a computational linguist who writes production code. Posted November 16. View posting
  • Applied ML Engineer, Localisation — Apple · Barcelona, Spain · compensation not listed
    Uses LLMs for data synthesis and translation on the Software Localisation team, which ships Apple software and Apple Intelligence features in other languages. You'd work from training through evaluation and deployment, alongside translation and localisation teams. Requires ML experience centered on LLMs, NLP and agentic workflows, and strong Python, Swift or C/C++. It's text and model localisation, not speech. View posting
  • Forward Deployed Engineer, Production — David AI · San Francisco (onsite) · $165K–$225K
    David AI records speech and audio datasets for AI labs. This role finds out whether a customer's model actually gets better on that data. You'd run ablations, trace a regression back to a recording setup, contributor pool or QA step, and change how the data is collected. Results come in days, not weeks. This is a different opening from the research engineer and audio engineer roles we listed on November 10. View posting
  • Translator / Linguist (Freelance) — ElevenLabs · Remote: UK, Spain, Germany, France, Portugal, Brazil, Mexico or India · per-task pay
    Edits AI-assisted translations and adapts scripts for dubbing, voiceover, subtitles and audiobooks in ElevenLabs' Productions marketplace. You'd work with the audio teams so translations sound natural when spoken. Requires native or near-native fluency and professional translation or localization experience. A background in linguistics or language technology is a bonus. It's freelance: you claim jobs on the platform. View posting
  • AI Voice Evaluation Specialist (3-month contract) — Innodata · Remote (Canada; a US version is also open) · CA$20–$25/hour (US version $20–$25)
    Holds the same role-played conversation with two voice AI models, records it, and rates each one on five quality dimensions. Then you write up which model did better and why. Requires a bachelor's degree, a good ear for small differences in how a conversation goes, and clear writing. It isn't a research job, but it's real experience judging speech models. Full time for three months. The Canada posting went up November 16. View posting

Speech research & models

    Joins Deepgram's research staff on the LLM side of its voice models: data curation, large-scale distributed training, transformer architecture and RL training, through to shipping models in production. The team's view is that audio data is too scarce and too high-dimensional to scale the usual way, so it's looking for new approaches. Requires extensive hands-on work on LLMs. Posted November 15. View posting
  • Research Scientist, Text-to-Speech — Cerence · Taipei, Taiwan or Tokyo, Japan · compensation not listed
    Builds next-generation TTS for in-car assistants and accessibility tools in major languages and dialects. That spans the front end (grapheme-to-phoneme, text normalization, polyphone disambiguation, prosody) and the back end (acoustic models like VITS, VALL-E or CosyVoice, and vocoders like HiFi-GAN), with inference tuned for edge devices and the cloud. Requires a master's, 2+ years of hands-on TTS development, and C/C++ and Python. The front-end work suits someone with phonetics training. View posting
  • Research Scientist / Senior Research Scientist, Japanese ASR — Cerence · Tokyo, Japan · compensation not listed
    Develops and tunes Japanese speech recognition for in-car voice assistants: training and fine-tuning, WER/CER and bad-case analysis, and Japanese-specific work on tokenizers, lexicons, text normalization, names, points of interest and addresses. Requires native or near-native Japanese (JLPT N1), fluent English, hands-on ASR experience and a master's. Conformer or Whisper-style model experience is preferred. View posting
