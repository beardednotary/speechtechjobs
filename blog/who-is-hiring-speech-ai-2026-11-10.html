Who's hiring in speech AI this week

November 10, 2026 • 22 roles

Twenty-two roles this week, and Apple accounts for ten of them. They span Siri Speech, speech-to-speech translation, generative voice research, speech enhancement and acoustics, in Cupertino, Boulder, Cambridge (UK), London, Barcelona and Copenhagen. Pay at the top is high elsewhere too: David AI is paying up to $360K for a research engineer who builds speech datasets for AI labs, xAI's Grok Voice team up to $357K, and Duolingo up to $331.2K for a staff speech recognition engineer. If you're in Europe, there are openings at Apple, PolyAI in the UK, Salesforce in Madrid and AssemblyAI in London. And if you don't write models, look at AssemblyAI's two pre-sales engineering roles (up to $300K OTE), Apple's speech program manager, and an audio engineer role at David AI.

Speech research & model engineering

  • Sr. Model Engineer, Voice — xAI · Palo Alto, CA · $220K–$357K + equity
    Builds the systems behind Grok Voice: pipelines that process real and synthetic audio, distributed training for speech-language models in JAX and PyTorch (including SFT and RL), and evals that track accuracy, audio quality and latency on every release. You'd also take models into real-time production. Requires a bachelor's in an engineering field, production code in Python, PyTorch, JAX, Rust or C++, and 2+ years on large-scale voice products. This is a separate, senior opening from the voice-model role we listed on September 29. View posting
  • Staff AI Research Engineer, Speech Recognition — Duolingo · New York, NY or Pittsburgh, PA · $220.8K–$331.2K
    Trains custom models that understand the speech of non-native learners, for features like Video Call. The work covers ASR, TTS, speech-to-speech and audio tokenization, across the whole stack: training data, fine-tuning, RL, evals, deployment and monitoring. Requires a proven record as an AI research engineer in speech recognition, TTS or LLMs, hands-on training of large models, an advanced degree (or equivalent experience) and leadership skills. View posting
  • Research Engineer — David AI · San Francisco (onsite) · $210K–$360K + equity
    Builds the algorithms and pipelines that turn large speech and audio collections into training datasets for AI labs: quality metrics, signal processing and ML to judge recordings, and filtering, deduplication and curation at scale. Requires 3+ years of engineering or applied research in areas like data systems, applied ML, or speech and audio. This is a different opening from the Research Scientist role we listed on September 22. View posting

Apple: Siri Speech, audio & acoustics

  • Machine Learning Engineer, Siri Speech — Apple · Cupertino, CA · $129.3K–$225.3K base
    Improves the multimodal models behind Siri's voice experiences. The posting lists an MS, 1–2+ years in ML, and experience with reinforcement learning and speech or multimodal LLMs, along with training or tuning large generative models in JAX or PyTorch. View posting
  • Sr. Machine Learning Research Engineer, Siri Speech — Apple · Cambridge, UK · compensation not listed
    Efficient machine learning for Siri's dialog systems and foundation models, from language understanding and multi-turn context to combining speech with text and other modalities. Requires papers at venues like ICASSP, Interspeech or NeurIPS, or a record of applying efficient deep learning in products, plus strong Python. View posting
  • Machine Learning Research, Speech-to-Speech Translation — Apple · Cambridge, UK · compensation not listed
    Builds end-to-end speech-to-speech translation: one model that listens, translates and speaks, with low latency and expressive output. Requires an MS or PhD, ML experience in speech recognition, machine translation, NLP or speech synthesis, and PyTorch, JAX or TensorFlow. Apple also has speech-to-speech translation research internships in Aachen and Cambridge. View posting
  • Machine Learning Researcher, Generative Audio — Apple · London, UK · compensation not listed
    Research on diffusion and flow-matching models for voice generation and speech synthesis, plus post-training and alignment, on the team behind Apple Music's AutoMix. Requires a PhD and papers at venues like ICASSP, ISMIR, Interspeech or NeurIPS. View posting

  • Acoustics ML & DSP Engineer — Apple · Boulder, CO · $134.7K–$249.5K base

Requires 5+ years in acoustics engineering, shipping high-volume consumer electronics with overseas manufacturers, transducer and MEMS design, and simulation in COMSOL or FEM/BEM tools. Up to 10% international travel. View posting
  • Engineering Program Manager, Speech Understanding & Generation — Apple · Cupertino, CA · $144.6K–$263.8K base
