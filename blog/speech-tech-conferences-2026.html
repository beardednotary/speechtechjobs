Conference Submission Tips

  • ICASSP: The most forgiving of the tier-1 venues — a good home for solid incremental work and system papers.
  • Interspeech: The most competitive venue for ASR; reviewers expect strong, well-run empirical results.
  • ASRU / SLT: Focus on practical systems — production deployments and real-world evaluations score well. ASRU runs in odd years, SLT in even years; for 2026 that means SLT 2026 in Palermo, not ASRU.
  • Timing: ICASSP's deadline is the earliest (~September the year before), then Interspeech (~February–March), then the odd/even-year workshop (~May–June) as a fallback.
  • Code/Data: Papers with public code and reproducible results tend to fare better in review.
