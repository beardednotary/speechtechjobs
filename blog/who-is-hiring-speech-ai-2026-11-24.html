Who's hiring in speech AI this week

November 24, 2026 • 19 roles

A voice agent is only useful once it can take a real phone call, and this week the companies building them are hiring the people who connect them to phone networks. Sierra wants a telephony field engineer who can read a packet capture and fix a dropped transfer, at up to $390K. Decagon is hiring for the same kind of work in London, Salesforce in Milan, and Motorola Solutions for an AI agent that answers non-emergency calls to 911 centers. There is also a run of audio software and DSP roles: spatial audio for Apple Vision Pro, audio for Intel's robotics platforms, and tiny speakers for Sesame's wearables. On the research side, Inflection AI is offering $400K–$550K base to lead its real-time voice work, and Speechmatics is hiring in London. Two roles this week require Spanish: Apple's Siri team in Barcelona and Retell AI in the Bay Area. Most of these roles went up this week. The rest have been open for a while and are in the digest for the first time.

Voice agents meet the phone network

Before a voice agent can answer a company's calls, someone has to connect it to that company's carriers, SIP trunks and contact center, and get it through their security review. These roles are for people who know telephony as well as AI. More on the jobs page.

  • Telephony Field Engineer — Sierra · San Francisco, CA (hybrid) · $280K–$390K plus equity
    Sierra's voice agents answer calls for Uber, Rivian, SoFi and Singapore Airlines. This role works out how each customer's calls reach the agent and get back to their contact center: SIP trunking, PSTN or websocket audio, transfers to human agents, and the IP ranges, codecs, TLS and SRTP details their security teams sign off on. The hardest deployments stay with you, such as customers who bring their own carrier or run an on-premises contact center. Requires 8+ years in field engineering for voice, telephony, CPaaS or contact-center products, and you must be able to find a problem in a packet capture. Posted November 19. View posting
  • Solutions Architect, Voice — Decagon · London, UK (in office) · £144.3K–£216.4K plus equity and commission
    A pre-sales role designing how Decagon's AI agents plug into enterprise contact centers such as Genesys, Amazon Connect, Cisco UCCE and Google CCAI. You'd design SIP integrations (bring-your-own-carrier trunking, REFER transfers, header propagation, media streaming) and trace multi-leg call flows down to the packet level. It's an early hire, so you'd also set how the team does this work. Posted in September and still open. View posting
  • Sr Software Engineer, Voice AI Agent Platform — Motorola Solutions · Remote in Canada (Ontario, Alberta or British Columbia) · compensation not listed
    Hyper, which Motorola Solutions acquired in April, builds an AI agent that handles non-emergency calls to 911 centers so that call-takers can focus on emergencies. As the team's senior technical lead you'd own the architecture of its real-time voice systems: low-latency audio, streaming, telephony, LLM orchestration and human-in-the-loop workflows, plus the evals and observability around them. You'd also lead incident response, and the posting is clear that humans still own what ships. View posting
  • Forward Deployed Engineer, Voice Agents Specialist — Salesforce · Milan, Italy · compensation not listed
    Builds and launches voice agents for customers on Agentforce Voice, Service Cloud Voice and contact-center telephony integrations, from discovery to go-live. You'd own the quality of each voice deployment: latency, call flow logic, interruption handling, multilingual performance and handoff to human agents. Requires 6+ years of software or technical delivery experience, including customer-facing delivery, hands-on LLM work, and about 25% travel. Posted November 19. View posting
  • Forward Deployed Engineer, Partnerships — AssemblyAI · San Francisco (hybrid) · $180K–$210K
    AssemblyAI's first engineer dedicated to partners. A growing share of its speech-to-text usage comes through LiveKit, Pipecat, Vapi, Telnyx, OpenRouter and the AWS and Databricks marketplaces. You'd make sure each new model works on those platforms when it launches, submit pull requests to partners' code, and run the WER, entity-accuracy and latency benchmarks that partners use to sell it. You'd write code most weeks. Posted November 17. View posting
  • Applied AI Engineer (Customer Facing), Spanish speaking — Retell AI · Redwood City, CA (in office) · $180K–$300K plus equity
    Builds and launches production voice agents with Retell's Spanish-speaking enterprise customers, and debugs their APIs, webhooks, SIP and latency problems, sometimes inside the customer's own code. Requires Spanish, a technical degree, and 2+ years of software engineering or customer-facing technical work. Retell says it passed $100M in annual revenue run rate this month. Posted November 17. View posting

Audio software, DSP & on-device

Audio software that ships inside hardware, from headsets and robots to wearables. Most of these roles ask for C/C++ and DSP fundamentals rather than ML. More on embedded voice AI jobs.

  • Applied ML Engineer, Edge Devices — Deepgram · Remote (US) · $155K–$245K plus 10% bonus
    This role works alongside the Embedded AI Engineer role we listed last week. That engineer writes the kernels. You adapt Deepgram's speech models to run on non-NVIDIA accelerators, edge servers and embedded platforms using the kernels and runtime each platform already has. That means swapping operators, choosing quantization and precision, rewriting graphs, and checking accuracy and latency on the real device. It isn't a research role. It suits someone who has already shipped models to non-GPU or edge hardware. Posted in September and still open. View posting
  • Audio Software Engineer, Vision Products Software — Apple · Boulder, CO · $165.8K–$292.4K base
    A senior role on the audio team for Apple Vision Pro, designing and building the software architecture for its spatial audio and mentoring other engineers. Requires 8+ years in industry, deep C/C++, test-driven development, and experience with audio architectures and DSP. AudioUnit plugin and embedded experience are preferred. Posted November 18. View posting
  • Audio Software Development Engineer, Apple Products — Apple · Bengaluru, India · compensation not listed
    Writes audio software that ships inside Apple products and runs on embedded platforms where real-time performance matters. The work covers algorithm design, implementation, tuning, debugging and production delivery, alongside hardware, firmware and tuning teams in other countries. Requires 5–7 years of embedded audio software, strong DSP, and C, C++ and Python. A master's in signal processing is preferred. Posted November 22. View posting
  • Audio System Software Engineer — Intel · Shanghai, China · compensation not listed
    Builds the Linux audio stack for Intel-based robots: ALSA, ASoC, Sound Open Firmware and PipeWire drivers, codec and smart-amplifier bring-up, echo cancellation, beamforming and microphone-array localization. It also covers deploying keyword spotting, wake-on-voice and ML noise suppression on Intel's NPUs with OpenVINO. Intel welcomes 2–7+ years of experience, so engineers with 2–3 years of embedded Linux can apply. Posted November 17. View posting
  • Speaker Systems Engineer — Sesame · San Francisco (onsite) · $175K–$280K
    Sesame is building wearables for its voice agents, and the speaker gets a fraction of a cubic centimeter. It has to be loud at the ear and quiet to everyone nearby. You'd own the speaker system end to end: picking transducers, designing the acoustic path, setting protection limits, reducing speaker-to-mic coupling with the DSP team, and testing at the factory. Requires 5+ years integrating speakers into consumer electronics and a shipped product where you owned the speaker. It's acoustics hardware, not ML. Posted in October and still open. View posting

Speech research & models

Research and model roles at speech-native companies, from ASR in London to a principal role leading real-time voice. More on ASR research jobs.

  • Senior Machine Learning Engineer — Speechmatics · London or Cambridge, UK · compensation not listed
    Joins the Modelling Team, which trains Speechmatics' speech recognition models, including large self-supervised ones, and puts them into production. The posting asks for a solid grasp of modern transformers (GQA, KV caching), distributed training, and inference optimization such as dynamic batching, flash attention and speculative decoding. Publications and open-source contributions are a plus. Posted in October and still open. View posting
  • Research Engineer — ElevenLabs · Remote (listed for Poland; offices in Warsaw, London, New York and San Francisco) · compensation not listed
    Joins ElevenLabs' research team to train and improve the audio models behind its voice, agent and creative products. The work includes new architectures, training methods, and benchmarks that show whether a new model is actually better. ElevenLabs doesn't require a degree. It asks for 3+ years training ML models and past projects or GitHub work that show you've solved hard problems. Posted in October and still open. View posting
  • Machine Learning Researcher, Audio — Bland AI · San Francisco · $160K–$280K plus equity and bonus
    Works across Bland's whole voice stack: expressive text-to-speech built on neural audio codecs, ASR that holds up to accents, phone-line noise and code-switching, the codecs themselves, and training pipelines for very large audio datasets. Bland's agents handle millions of calls a day, and you'd take ideas from experiment to production inference. Posted in April and still open. View posting
  • Principal Research & Engineering, Realtime Voice AI — Inflection AI · Palo Alto, CA · $400K–$550K base
    A hands-on lead who sets the roadmap for Inflection's real-time voice stack: streaming ASR, TTS, speech-to-speech, speech LLMs, turn-taking, barge-in and latency, on a 1,000-GPU cluster. You'd decide what to build, buy or train, build evals that measure voice quality beyond WER (emotional fit, interruption handling, task success), and mentor a team. Requires experience leading production voice or conversational AI systems. Posted in June and still open. View posting
  • Machine Learning Engineer, Inference & Serving (Speech LLM) — Plaud · San Francisco (hybrid) · $195K–$365K base plus bonus and equity
    Plaud makes AI note-taking recorders and says it has more than 3 million users. This role builds the low-latency inference engines for its speech models and LLMs. The posting asks for experience with time-to-first-audio trade-offs, continuous batching, KV cache management (PagedAttention) and stateful real-time connections, plus a deep understanding of NVIDIA Ampere and Hopper GPUs. Posted in May and still open. View posting
  • Software Engineer, ML Infrastructure — Sesame · San Francisco (onsite) · $175K–$280K
    Sesame trains and serves its own speech and conversational models. This role builds the GPU platform they run on: cluster scheduling when there aren't enough GPUs for every job, resuming interrupted runs, where training data and checkpoints live, and finding why a training run is slow. It starts on the training side and covers serving too. Posted November 23. View posting

Speech data & language

Two roles where knowing the language or the data matters as much as modeling. More on spoken language jobs.

  • Language Engineer (Mexican Spanish), Global Siri — Apple · Barcelona, Spain · compensation not listed
    Global Siri teaches Siri each new language, from speech recognition and language understanding to the response. This role is the technical lead for Mexican Spanish, building ML and generative AI systems so that Siri feels native to users in Mexico. Requires professional fluency in Mexican Spanish, applied ML and NLP experience, production coding, and work with data at scale. ASR, TTS or pronunciation-modeling experience is preferred. Posted November 18. View posting
  • Senior Data Scientist, Audio — Deepgram · Remote (US) · $165K–$220K plus equity and bonus
    Works out what is in Deepgram's audio data (languages, domains, speakers, recording conditions, quality) and what is missing. You'd build active-learning loops that decide what to label next, design how human reviewers fit in, and build the benchmark sets behind Deepgram's accuracy claims. Requires strong Python, data-selection or active-learning work, and a working knowledge of speech or NLP model errors. ASR/TTS, multilingual or code-switched data and LLM-assisted annotation are pluses. Posted in September and still open. View posting
