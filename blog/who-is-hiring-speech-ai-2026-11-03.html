Who's hiring in speech AI this week

November 3, 2026 • 21 roles

Twenty-one roles this week. The best pay you can earn remotely is at Deepgram, which is paying $216K–$338K for US-remote researchers who take a customer's voice-AI problem, fix it in the models, and ship the fix within weeks. Amazon's Fauna Robotics is hiring three audio specialists at once, a principal scientist, an acoustic engineer and an embedded DSP lead, to work out how a home robot hears over its own motors. Otter.ai wants a director of AI research for ASR, speaker identification and audio language models at up to $313K. If you're early in your career, look at Plaud in Singapore and Audible in Culver City: both ask for just a year of experience. Cartesia is also hiring a contractor to turn voice-actor recordings into its TTS voices.

Speech & audio research

    Finds where Deepgram's speech-to-text and TTS models fall short for customers and fixes it in production within weeks. The posting's examples include new languages, TTS pronunciation of names and numbers, isolating a speaker from crosstalk, and steerable and cloned voices. You'd set the targets, design evals, curate or generate data and fine-tune. Requires deep experience training and evaluating deep learning models, strong PyTorch, and ML you've shipped to production. STT, TTS or voice-agent experience is a plus, not a requirement. View posting
  • Research Engineer, Post-Training — Deepgram · Remote (US), San Francisco or New York · $216K–$338K + bonus + equity
    A second opening on the same team, with the same description and pay as the role above but an engineering title. Apply to whichever title fits your background. View posting
  • Principal Applied Scientist, Real-Time Conversational AI — Amazon (AGI) · Sunnyvale, CA ($228.7K–$309.4K) or Boston or Seattle ($198.9K–$269K) · + sign-on + RSUs
    Sets the technical direction for real-time speech and audio foundation models, along with the reward modeling and RL that shape natural conversational behavior. This is the principal-level version of the senior role we listed last week, posted October 28. Requires a PhD, 10+ years of applied research, and hands-on work scaling foundation models that process or generate speech, text and audio together. View posting
  • Director of AI Research — Otter.ai · Mountain View, CA · $244K–$313K
    A hands-on research leader for ASR, speaker identification, LLMs and audio language models. You'd own the whole model lifecycle, from data and pre-training to evaluation and deployment, and build the team. Requires a master's or higher (PhD preferred), 10+ years in software or AI research including 4+ managing technical teams, publications and deployed systems, and deep expertise in both speech and LLMs. View posting
  • Research Scientist II, Deepfake Detection & Authentication (Video & Audio) — Pindrop · Remote (US) · $160K–$185K + RSUs
    Researches how to detect synthetic speech and manipulated video, plus voice and identity authentication, at a voice-security company that says it analyzes more than 8 billion interactions a year. Requires a master's or PhD (or equivalent research experience), 3+ years of deep-learning research in speech, vision or voice biometrics, and hands-on deepfake detection work backed by papers or patents. ASVspoof or NIST SRE experience is a plus. View posting
  • Research Scientist (all levels) — Pindrop · Remote (US) · compensation not listed
    Pindrop's general research posting, open at every level from 1–3 years of experience to senior and principal (6+). It covers fraud, voice and speech, video, authentication and deepfake detection. Requires a master's or PhD (or equivalent research experience), strong PyTorch or TensorFlow, and a record of delivered or published research. Apply here if you're earlier in your career than the Research Scientist II role above. View posting
  • Research Staff, LLMs — Deepgram · Remote (EMEA) · compensation not listed
    An LLM specialist for Deepgram's research team, working on the hard parts: data curation, large-scale distributed training, transformer architecture optimization and RL. The team's stated goal is new ways to build voice AI that get around audio's scarcity and cost problems. One of the few listings this week open to people based in Europe, the Middle East or Africa.
